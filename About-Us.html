About AscendShip

We believe everyone deserves a fair first step into tech — whether you're a student, a fresh graduate or switching careers.

Our mission

Most job boards are built for people who already have experience. Juniors end up sending hundreds of applications to roles that ask for “3+ years”, and never hear back.

AscendShip was created to change that. We only list internships and junior roles from companies that are ready to train new people, we match them to your skills, and we surround you with a community and mentors who have been in your shoes.

Frequently asked questions

Is AscendShip free for candidates?+

Yes. Creating an account, applying, joining events and talking to mentors is completely free.

Do I need experience to apply?+

No. All opportunities on AscendShip are internships, entry-level or junior roles. Personal and school projects count as experience.

How does the matching work?+

When you sign up you select your skills. For each opportunity we calculate how many of the required skills you have and show it as a “% match”. You can update your skills anytime from your profile.

I'm switching careers. Is AscendShip for me?+

Absolutely! Many of our members come from other fields. Check out the QA and support roles — they are great entry points.

My company wants to post jobs. How?+

Send us a message using the contact form below and choose “Partnership”. Our team will get back to you within 2 business days.

Privacy policy

AscendShip is a student project. All the data you enter (account, applications, posts, messages) is stored only in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.

Passwords are never stored in plain text — we keep only a SHA-256 hash of them.

You can delete your account and all related data at any time from your profile page. Clearing your browser data also removes everything.
